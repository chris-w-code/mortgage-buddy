    How this works: with the 5% deposit scheme, all your cash (house savings plus other accounts) can't be more than the hold-back limit (months × expenses plus repayment). Savings above that go into the deposit, which lowers the loan. At a 20% deposit or more you don't need the scheme, so the limit doesn't apply. Stamp duty uses 2026 NSW rates, with the first home buyer discount phasing out between $800k and $1M. Fortnightly means half the monthly amount every fortnight. Check your bank sets it this way.
